## 1. Takeaways

1. **“Dry January” is the monster seasonal keyword** — not a year-round acquisition engine. US avg ~9.9k but **Jan 2026 ≈ 74k**; UK avg ~5.4k, **Jan ≈ 33k**. Plan content/ASO for Dec–Jan.  
2. **Brand competitors already own search:** “reframe app” ~4.4k US; “i am sober” ~3.6k US. We need product-led ASO + seasonal SEO, not head-to-head brand bids early.  
3. **Category intent that fits us:** sober/sobriety app (~2.9k), quit alcohol (~2.9k), quit drinking (~1.9k), sober curious (~2.9k).  
4. **Proof-angle keywords:** “alcohol calories” ~1.6k US, **very low CPC (~$0.03)** — content gold for twin-gains positioning.  
5. **Tracker phrases are tiny** (“alcohol tracker” ~210 US) — people search outcomes (“quit”, “sober app”), not “tracker.”
