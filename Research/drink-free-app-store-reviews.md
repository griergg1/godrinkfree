## 3. Competitor-by-competitor

### Reframe — high stars, fragile trust
- **Love:** science-backed plans, tracking, community, Dry January results stories.  
- **Hate:** billing opacity, hard cancel, annual surprise, coaching price stacking.  
- **Implication for Drink Free:** Win reviews with **radical billing clarity** (price on store + before account), freemium that isn’t a hostage counter, no “vulnerable then upsell” flow.

### I Am Sober — category volume king (but not alcohol-only)
- **Love:** free core, second-by-second progress, daily pledge, community, fair Plus price.  
- **Hate:** community moderation; Plus creep; multi-addiction dilution.  
- **Implication:** Match **free useful core**; specialize in **alcohol twin gains** (money + kcal) they underplay.

### Try Dry — beloved free utility
- **Love:** free, drank-as-planned, money/calories/units, Dry January legitimacy.  
- **Hate:** polish/bugs under peak load.  
- **Implication:** Don’t compete on free-only; compete on **year-round game loop + iOS craft**.

### Sunnyside — coaching premium
- **Love:** mindful drinking, plans, coaches, community prompts.  
- **Hate:** subscription cost.  
- **Implication:** Stay visual/in-app cheaper; coaching is Phase 2+.

### Smoke Free — why reviews feel “cleaner”
- Binary quit story; long tenure; Pro framed as worth it; clinical/advisor legitimacy; less “scam billing” narrative in public writeups than Reframe.  
- Loyalty reviews (“years later I still check money saved”).

## 4. Review-driven product rules for Drink Free

1. Show **price before** deep personal disclosure.  
2. Free tier keeps **streak + money + calories** useful forever.  
3. Pro = missions depth / charts / vault / widgets — not oxygen.  
4. Cancel instructions in-app + email receipt.  
5. Community optional and moderated — or delay community.  
6. Chase **5★ themes**: life change, clear numbers, fair price, not alone — without Reframe’s billing scar.
