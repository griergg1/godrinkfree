## 2. Deep sketches

### Reframe (category revenue leader)
- **Strengths:** Brand search (~4.4k US/mo), curriculum depth, coaching ladder, Dry January data PR machine, VC-backed scale narrative (~$13M ARR claims / 150k paid — treat as directional).  
- **Weaknesses:** Heavy / expensive feel; paywall friction; less “fintech fun.”  
- **Vs us:** We win on **immediate dopamine dashboard** (charts, badges, points); lose on content depth and coaching until much later.

### I Am Sober
- **Strengths:** Simple streak product; strong brand KW (~3.6k); free tier acquisition; community pledges.  
- **Weaknesses:** Generic multi-addiction; thinner money/calorie theatre; less dual-track sophistication.  
- **Vs us:** Match streak simplicity; differentiate with **£ + kcal gains** and quit/cut-back modes.

### Try Dry (Alcohol Change UK)
- **Strengths:** Official Dry January gravity; free; units + money + calories already; “drank as planned.”  
- **Weaknesses:** Seasonal mindshare; not a gamey year-round habit product; charity UX.  
- **Vs us:** Borrow honesty of logging model; beat on **daily retention game loop** and iOS polish.

### Sunnyside
- **Strengths:** Moderation positioning; SMS accountability; clear paid coaching value.  
- **Weaknesses:** Pricey tiers; less visual “portfolio of me.”  
- **Vs us:** Stay in-app visual; optional SMS later.

### Clinical (Vorvida, Monument, Ria, Oar)
- Different game: diagnosis codes, Rx, reimbursement.  
- **Do not compete** on MVP claims; learn safety copy only.
