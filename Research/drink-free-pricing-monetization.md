**Date:** 10 Aug 2026  
**Status:** Hypothesis — validate on waitlist  
**Related:** competitor · market · features MVP

## 1. What the market charges (desk research)

Prices move often (trials, geo, promo). Ranges observed in 2025–2026 reviews / store listings:

| Product | Model | Typical price points (USD) | Notes |
|---------|--------|----------------------------|--------|
| **Reframe** | Subscription + coaching | Listed ~$10–20/mo; ~$100–140/yr common; coaching add-ons up to ~$250 | Multiple SKUs in App Store; 7-day trial common |
| **I Am Sober Plus** | Freemium | ~$5–10/mo or ~$40–120/yr (promo vs list) | Free core is the wedge |
| **Sunnyside** | Tiered sub | ~$12/mo → ~$99/yr; premium tiers higher (~$36/mo / ~$299/yr cited) | SMS/coaching justifies price |
| **Try Dry** | Free | $0 | Charity; anchors “tracker should be free” |
| **Nomo / others** | Low IAP | ~$3–10/mo class | Commodity counter |

**Insight:** Paying for **program + coaching** clears $100+/yr. Paying for **counter-only** is hard — Try Dry is free. Drink Free must sell **ongoing game loop + twin gains + missions**, not a day counter.

## 2. Monetization options for Drink Free

| Model | Pros | Cons | Fit |
|-------|------|------|-----|
| **A. Freemium + Pro sub** | Matches I Am Sober; Jan acquisition | Need clear Pro value | **Primary recommendation** |
| **B. Soft paywall after trial** | Higher ARPU like Reframe | Review backlash; needs wow | Secondary if retention strong |
| **C. One-time unlock** | Simple | No recurring; updates unpaid | Weak for missions content |
| **D. Coaching upsell** | High ARPU | Ops cost; regulatory tone | Phase 2+ |
| **E. B2B / wellness** | Stable contracts | Long sales | Phase 2+ |
| **F. DiGA / clinical** | Reimbursement | Expensive evidence | Explicit Phase 3 / no |

## 3. Recommended packaging (v1 hypothesis)

### Free
- Dual-track onboarding  
- Streak / budget basics  
- Money + calories (core twin gains)  
- Limited missions (e.g. 1/day)  
- Basic badges  

### Pro (subscription)
- Full mission queue + history  
- Advanced charts (7d / 30d / all)  
- Treat vault  
- Extra badge seasons / levels  
- Custom drink presets beyond defaults  
- Widgets + richer reminders  

### Not in paid day one
- Human coaching  
- Clinical claims  

### Price hypothesis (USD / GBP)

| Plan | Price | Rationale |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Pro monthly | **$7.99 / £6.99** | Below Reframe monthly feel; above cheap counter IAPs |
| Pro annual | **$49.99 / £39.99** | Near I Am Sober Plus promo band; easy “less than a round a month” |
| Intro | 7-day Pro trial **or** 14-day free Pro during Dry January campaign | Seasonality |

**Positioning line:** *“Less than one pint a month — keep the money and the calories.”*

## 4. Unit economics sketch (illustrative)

Assumptions to pressure-test — **not forecasts**:

| Input | Example |
|-------|---------|
| Organic + seasonal CPI | $0–$8 blended (Jan paid higher) |
| Free → Pro convert (90d) | 3–8% |
| Annual attach | Prefer push annual |
| Gross after store cut | ~85% (Apple) |
| Churn | High post-Jan unless missions stick |

**Rough:** 10,000 installs → 400 Pro (4%) → 250 annual @ $50 → ~$12.5k gross → ~$10.6k after Apple — enough to learn, not a business yet. Scale needs Jan waves + retention.

---

## 5. Paywall UX principles

1. Show twin gains **before** paywall (value visible).  
2. Never lock crisis / safety links.  
3. Free streak must remain useful (avoid “hostage counter”).  
4. Pro sells **depth of game** (missions, vault, charts), not oxygen.  
5. January: longer trial; February: convert with “keep your gains” narrative.

## 8. Working decision

**Ship freemium.** Price Pro around **$8/mo or $50/yr**. Differentiate on gamey twin gains so we’re not competing with free Try Dry on “tracker only,” and not competing with Reframe on coaching ARPU until we earn it.
