## 3. On-page template (every post)

1. **H1** = title with primary KW near front  
2. **Intro (50–80 words):** promise + who it’s for + dual-track nod  
3. **Jump links** if long  
4. **H2s** that mirror People Also Ask  
5. **One interactive or static table** (kcal table / savings example)  
6. **Soft CTA box:** “Track money + calories in Drink Free — waitlist”  
7. **FAQ schema** (3–5 Qs)  
8. **Internal links:** 2 pillar posts + homepage  
9. **Disclaimer:** not medical advice / not a medical device  

Length target: **1,200–1,800 words** for pillars; **800–1,200** for seasonal tactics.
