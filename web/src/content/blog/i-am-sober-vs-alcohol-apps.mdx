I Am Sober is one of the most downloaded habit trackers in the world. It works for alcohol, vaping, sugar, or any streak-based goal. That flexibility is its strength — and sometimes its limitation.

## What I Am Sober does well

- Clean day counter and streaks
- Daily pledge and check-in
- Community for many habits
- Good free core and optional IAP
- Reminders and widgets

If you want a simple “I am X days sober” experience, it’s hard to beat.

## Where alcohol-specific apps add value

Alcohol has its own math: units, calories, prices, weekly budgets, and social pressure. A dedicated alcohol app can give you:

- Drink-by-drink logging
- Money saved tied to your actual prices
- Calories not added by drink type
- Weekly cut-back budgets (not just zero)
- Proof of what skipping a round really earns

## Comparing the two philosophies

| I Am Sober | Alcohol-specific app |
|------------|----------------------|
| Streak identity | Twin gains identity |
| Cross-habit support | Drink-specific math |
| Community | Solo dashboard |
| Great for quit | Good for quit or cut-back |

Neither is wrong. Different stages need different tools.

## When to use I Am Sober

- Early days: you need a clear number of days
- Community helps you stay accountable
- You want widgets on your home screen
- You track multiple habits in one place

## When to add an alcohol-specific app

- You want to track money and calories as motivation
- You’re cutting back, not quitting, and need a budget
- You want to compare dry periods financially
- You’re choosing between several drinks and want a “kcal per serve” scoreboard
