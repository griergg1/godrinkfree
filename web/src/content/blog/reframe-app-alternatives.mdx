Reframe is a popular alcohol-reduction app. For some people the daily neuroscience lessons, community, and structured program are exactly what they need. For others, it’s more than they want — and they’d rather just log, budget, and watch twin gains stack.

This guide is for the second group.

## What Reframe does well

- Daily lessons and check-ins
- Community support and accountability
- Structured path toward reduction or abstinence
- Strong habit-formation scaffolding

If that structure works for you, keep using it. If it feels like homework, read on.

## What a lighter alternative looks like

A lighter app usually has:

- **No lesson queue** — open, log, close
- **Flexible cut-back budgets** — not just quit or all-in
- **Live scoreboard** — money saved, calories not added, streaks
- **Missions / points** — gamey, not clinical
- **No surprise billing** — Free vs Pro before you start

The goal is to reduce friction, not to replace therapy.

## Who benefits from a lighter tracker

- Already in therapy or support elsewhere
- Wants to measure Dry January, Damp January, or a 30-day test
- Finds daily lessons feel like guilt pressure
- Prefers to experiment with quit vs cut-back week by week

## How to move without losing progress

1. Export or screenshot your numbers (drinks, streak, savings).
2. Pick a start date and a new weekly budget.
3. Set your usual drink and price in the new app.
4. Log the first week carefully so the scoreboard feels honest.
5. Decide later if you want more structure again.

## Where Drink Free fits

Drink Free is a **lighter, gamey tracker** with:

- Quit **or** cut-back mode
- Money saved + calories not added
- Missions for **points** (not XP)
- Clear Free / Pro pricing
- No medical program — just a habit companion

If Reframe felt like a course, Drink Free is a dashboard.
