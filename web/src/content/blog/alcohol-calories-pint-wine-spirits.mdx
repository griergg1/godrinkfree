Alcohol calories are the stealth snack of the night out. You track dinner. You forget the pint. Then wonder why the belt feels different by Thursday.

This guide is for anyone quitting **or** cutting back who wants a clear kcal scoreboard — not a lecture. Drink Free treats **calories not added** as a twin gain next to money saved.

## Why alcohol calories sneak up on you

Liquid calories don’t fill you up the way food does. A “couple of drinks” can match a full meal before you notice.

Three sneak patterns:

- **Round math** — “Just one more” is often three.
- **Serve creep** — 175ml wine becomes 250ml; a single becomes a double.
- **Mixer tax** — spirits look light until cola, juice, or syrup joins.

If you can see the kcal, you can play the game: skip, swap, or budget.

## Beer vs wine vs spirits (same night, different kcal)

Same social night, three menus:

| Night (4 drinks) | Rough kcal |
|------------------|------------|
| 4 × lager pint | ~728 |
| 4 × wine 175ml | ~632 |
| 4 × spirit + mixer (single) | ~480 |
| 4 × cocktails | ~880 |

None of these are “good” or “bad.” They’re **inputs**. If your goal is cut-back, swapping two cocktails for singles (or skipping the last round) is a concrete mission — and it shows up as calories not added.

## “Calories not added” if you skip 5 drinks a week

House-mix average in Drink Free starts around **180 kcal** per drink if you haven’t set a usual yet.

Skip **5 drinks/week** → about **900 kcal/week** not added → roughly **46,800 kcal/year**.

That’s not a promise about weight. It’s a scoreboard number you can feel. Stack it next to cash saved and the night starts looking like a game, not a guilt spiral.
