“Quit drinking” searches often hide a quieter question: **what happens to the money?**

This post is for people who want a calculator vibe without a spreadsheet tab they’ll abandon by March. Quit **or** cut back — both produce savings. Drink Free charts them as wallet gains next to calories not added.

## Quick math: drinks/week × price

**Weekly spend ≈ drinks per week × usual price**

**Money saved ≈ drinks skipped × usual price**

Examples with a **£5.50** pint:

| Skipped / week | Weekly | Year |
|----------------|--------|------|
| 3 | £16.50 | ~£858 |
| 7 | £38.50 | ~£2,002 |
| 14 | £77 | ~£4,004 |

Bump the price to a **£10** night out and the year number jumps hard. That’s why personalising the price matters more than borrowing someone else’s “I saved £5k” screenshot.

## UK pint vs US cocktail examples

**UK — after-work pints**

- 4 pints/week × £5.50 → **£22/week** → **~£1,144/year** if you quit that habit fully.

**US — cocktail nights**

- 2 nights × 3 cocktails × $14 → **$84/week** → **~$4,368/year**.

Same social pattern, different currency, same scoreboard idea: make the skip visible.

## What people do with the money (treat vault)

Savings stick better when they become a **treat**, not a void.

Ideas that fit a gamey vault:

- Monthly dinner that isn’t “just because we didn’t drink”
- Headphones / hobby fund
- Trip deposit that grows every dry weekend
- Emergency buffer you can actually see rising

Drink Free’s direction is a treat vault unlocked with **points** from missions — so cash saved isn’t abstract; it’s fuel for something you want.
