Dry January creates a seasonal rush of app downloads. But the right app depends on whether you want a 31-day sprint or a year-round habit change.

This guide compares the main options and what to keep after January.

## Try Dry

**Best for:** Dry January purists, UK-focused users, charity participation.

Try Dry is the official app from Alcohol Change UK. It’s purpose-built for the month, with daily trackers, quizzes, and community stats. It’s strongest in January and during the charity’s campaigns.

Year-round, it’s thinner than apps built for long-term tracking.

## Reframe

**Best for:** People who want a structured daily program.

Reframe combines lessons, neuroscience, and community. It’s heavier than a tracker, which is great if you want coaching, but overkill if you just want a budget and a chart.

## I Am Sober

**Best for:** Streak identity and cross-habit support.

Clean day counter, widgets, daily motivation, and a large community. For alcohol, it’s excellent for quit mode but less built for cut-back or money/calorie math.

## Sunnyside

**Best for:** Mindful drinking and cut-back.

Sunnyside-style apps focus on drink planning, weekly limits, and gentle reminders. Good for Damp January and people who don’t want to quit fully.

## Drink Free (waitlist)

**Best for:** Twin gains and gamey tracking.

Money saved + calories not added, missions for **points**, quit or cut-back. Free / Pro clearly stated. Designed to keep the January momentum after February starts.

## Year-round checklist

Whatever you choose, look for:

- Clear Free / Pro pricing
- Weekly budgets (not just streaks)
- Money + calorie tracking
- Soft reset after a slip
- Honest, non-medical positioning
