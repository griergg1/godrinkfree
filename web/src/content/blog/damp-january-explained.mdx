Dry January gets the headlines. **Damp January** gets the people who don’t want to quit, but do want a reset.

It’s the cut-back version of the season: fewer drinks, clearer rules, and a full month of practicing moderation without a lifetime vow.

## What Damp January means

Instead of zero drinks, you pick a clear weekly limit. Common versions:

- Max 3 drinks per week
- Only drink on weekends
- No drinking on consecutive nights
- One drink per social event

The rule is personal. The only requirement is that it’s clear enough to track.

## Why Damp January works for some people

All-or-nothing can feel like a setup. Damp January gives you:

- Permission to participate socially
- Practice at saying “just one”
- A softer identity test
- Still meaningful money and calorie savings

It’s also a great dry-run for a longer cut-back year.

## How to set a weekly drink budget

1. Count your honest baseline from last month.
2. Pick a 50% cut to start, or a hard cap like 7 drinks/week.
3. Assign drinks to specific days (e.g., 2 on Friday, 2 on Saturday).
4. Track each drink as it happens.
5. Review weekly and adjust.

## Social scripts

- “I’m doing Damp January — one drink max tonight.”
- “I’m cutting back this month; soda after this.”
- “I’m trying a weekly budget, so I’m saving my drinks for later.”

Clarity makes it easier for others to support you.

## What you still gain

Even a damp month produces real twin gains:

- Fewer calories not added
- Lower spend
- Better sleep most nights
- Proof that moderation is possible
