**Sober curious** means you’re questioning alcohol’s default role — without needing a clinical identity first.

This is for people who aren’t sure about “alcoholic” labels, still want a social life, and prefer experiments over oaths. Drink Free’s dual track (quit **or** cut back) is built for that middle.

## Definition without AA pressure

Sober curious ≈ *I’m curious what life feels like with less (or no) alcohol — and I’m allowed to learn.*

It is **not**:

- A diagnosis
- A requirement to attend meetings
- A forever decision on day one

It **is** permission to run tests: dry weekends, Dry-ish months, or a weekly budget.

## Sober curious vs sobriety vs mindful drinking

| Idea | Core move | Typical rule |
|------|-----------|--------------|
| Sober curious | Experiment & notice | Flexible |
| Sobriety | Abstinence identity / recovery path | Often zero |
| Mindful drinking | Attention while drinking | Fewer, slower, intentional |

You can borrow tools from all three. Drink Free stays non-clinical: habit companion, twin gains, no lecture.

## Experiments to try for 30 days

Pick one. Write it down. Treat it like a season, not a personality transplant.

1. **Zero on weeknights** — weekends unchanged at first.
2. **Half baseline** — if you averaged 10, budget 5.
3. **Two-drink ceiling** any night you drink.
4. **Social alternate** — every other outing dry.
5. **Full dry 30** — quit mode if you want a clean chart.

Track **money saved** and **calories not added** so the experiment has a scoreboard. Curiosity loves data.

## Social scripts for nights out

Keep them short:

- “I’m on a challenge this month.”
- “Driving / early start tomorrow.”
- “I’m good with a soda — still here for the chat.”
- “Cutting back for a bit — don’t make it weird.”

If someone pushes, repeat once and change topic. You don’t owe a TED Talk.

## Tools that help (tracking without shame)

Look for:

- Free or clear freemium (no surprise billing)
- Quit **and** cut-back
- Visible gains, not only day counters
- Missions / **points** that feel light

Avoid tools that make one slip feel like deletion. Soft resets keep curious people in the game.
