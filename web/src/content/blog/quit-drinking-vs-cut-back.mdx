Searching **cut back alcohol** or **quit alcohol** usually means you’re already negotiating with yourself.

This guide helps you pick a starting mode — quit **or** cut back — without locking you in forever. Drink Free is built dual track on purpose.

## Signs cut-back is enough for now

Cut-back often fits if:

- You enjoy some drinks and want a **ceiling**, not a ban
- Weekends blow the budget but weeknights are fine
- All-or-nothing has failed you before
- You want practice saying no *sometimes* before saying no *always*

Cut-back is still a goal. It’s not “failure to quit.” It’s a weekly drink budget with green gains when you come in under it.

## Signs a full quit fits better

A quit streak can fit better if:

- Moderation turns into bargaining every time
- Health, sleep, or relationships need a clean break
- You’re in a Dry January / challenge window and want a clear rule
- Counting drinks feels harder than counting zeros

Quit mode is simple: drinks skipped = full twin gains. No debate mid-round.

## You can switch later (dual track)

Identity labels (“I’m sober” vs “I’m mindful”) can wait. Skills transfer:

- Logging
- Seeing money + calories
- Missions for **points**
- Social scripts

Start where you’ll actually play. Switch when the old mode stops working.

## Why all-or-nothing fails some people

All-or-nothing fails when one slip deletes the whole story. Then shame → spiral → “I’ll start Monday.”

A cut-back budget survives a slip: you’re still in the game if the week can recover. A quit streak can restart without drama too — if the app treats it like a run, not a verdict.

## How to set a weekly drink budget

1. **Baseline** — honest drinks last 2 weeks (no hero edits).
2. **Target** — pick a number 20–40% lower to start (or zero for quit).
3. **Shape** — e.g. max 2 on Fri, 2 on Sat, 0 elsewhere.
4. **Price + kcal** — attach your usual drink so twin gains update.
5. **Review Sunday** — tighten, hold, or switch to quit.

| Mode | Rule | What you celebrate |
|------|------|--------------------|
| Quit | 0 drinks | Streak + full savings/kcal |
| Cut-back | Weekly max | Under-budget nights + partial gains |
