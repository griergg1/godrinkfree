Dry January is one of the biggest alcohol-related search moments of the year. The problem isn’t starting — it’s finishing. Most people fail in the first two weekends.

Here’s a simple plan to make it to 31 January with a full month of twin gains.

## Pre-January: set the rules

Before 1 January, write down:

- Goal: fully dry, or damp with a weekly limit
- Your usual drinks and prices
- A clear reason you want this
- One treat you’ll buy with the money saved

No rules in writing means mid-month bargaining wins.

## Week 1 — novelty week

You’re motivated. Stack wins early:

- Remove alcohol from home if possible
- Tell close friends you’re doing it
- Log every night you skip
- Watch the first money/calories numbers appear

Week 1 is your proof of concept. Make it visible.

## Week 2 — the first weekend

This is the danger zone. Have a plan:

- Social script ready: “I’m doing Dry Jan, tonic for me.”
- Bring a non-alcoholic option to parties
- Plan a Friday night that doesn’t revolve around pubs
- Track the weekend as a mini-challenge inside the month

If you survive the first weekend, odds improve dramatically.

## Week 3 — the plateau

Motivation dips. Rely on systems:

- Daily check-in in the app
- Count total money saved so far
- Revisit the treat you promised yourself
- One social event, planned sober

## Week 4 — the finish line

You’re close. Don’t coast.

- Keep the same rules as week 1
- Celebrate with the saved money, not a drink
- Decide what you’ll do in February before February decides for you

## If you slip

One drink ≠ one failed month. The research is clear: slip-and-recover beats all-or-nothing. Log it, reset the next day, and finish the month anyway.
